# ISO/IEC 27701

OSM is **not** a PIMS and not a claim of ISO/IEC 27701
certification.

ISO/IEC 27701 extends ISO/IEC 27001/27002 with privacy information
management guidance for PII controllers and processors. OSM can
carry a small set of privacy facts on a technological service or
offering without becoming a Record of Processing Activities.

## What maps

| OSM field | Where | Role |
|-----------|--------|------|
| `mappings.iso27701` | Technology Stack | Free-text ISO 27701 references, or `not-applicable` |
| `iso27701_pii_role` | Offering posture | `controller` / `processor` / `joint-controller` / `none` |
| `iso27701_pii_categories` | Offering posture | Categories of personal data |
| `iso27701_retention_days` | Offering posture | Retention characterization |
| `data_classification` | Service posture | Classification of data the service handles |
| `security_classification` | Service posture | Security sensitivity of the service itself |

## What stays in ISO 27701

- organization-level privacy roles
- processing purposes and data-subject rights operations
- a full privacy information management system

Enumerating PII categories is not a legal basis, DPIA, or transfer
assessment.
