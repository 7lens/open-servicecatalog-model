# ISO/IEC 27001

**Mapped version:** **ISO/IEC 27001:2022** (Edition 3) **+
Amendment 1:2024** (climate-action wording in clauses 4.1 / 4.2).
Amendment 1 does **not** change Annex A. Superseded: ISO/IEC
27001:2013.

> **Mapping basis (2026-06-11):** Amendment 1:2024 adds a sentence at
> the end of clause 4.1 asking organizations to assess whether climate
> change is relevant, and a note at clause 4.2 that interested parties
> may hold climate-related requirements; the overall intent of 4.1 and
> 4.2 is unchanged and Annex A is not altered
> ([climate-change amendment summary — Advisera](https://advisera.com/articles/climate-change-amendment-in-iso-standards/),
> [NQA climate-change update](https://www.nqa.com/en-ca/resources/news/iso-update-climatechange)).
> The ISO 27001 full text is paywalled; the amendment scope above is
> taken from secondary guidance and the public preview of
> [ISO/IEC 27001:2022/Amd 1:2024](https://www.iso.org/standard/88435.html).
> Source wording was rephrased for licensing compliance.

**Status:** **MAPPED** (control ID locators only)

OSM is **not** an ISMS, a Statement of Applicability, or a claim of
ISO/IEC 27001 certification. OSM can **map** catalog-level control
references onto a technological service. It does not implement
Annex A controls, SoA objects, control-implementation objects, or
certification evidence.

## What stays in ISO 27001 (**EXTERNAL**)

- the organization-level ISMS
- Statement of Applicability (SoA)
- control implementation objects
- certification evidence structures
- risk treatment plans

Listing control IDs is not evidence that controls are implemented.
Example Annex A IDs are illustrative only.
